About Us

Fits by Rich Aunty Dam’s (RAD) is a luxury fashion brand based in Abuja, Nigeria, dedicated to creating stylish bespoke and ready-to-wear outfits for men, women, and children. Founded by Akinlabi Oyindamola Deborah, RAD was built from a passion for creativity, elegance, and quality craftsmanship. With over three years of tailoring experience, the brand has become known for detailed sewing, clean finishing, and designs that combine comfort, confidence, and sophistication. At RAD, every outfit is carefully crafted to reflect individuality and style while maintaining a high standard of excellence. From custom-made pieces to ready-to-wear fashion, our goal is to help clients look and feel their best for every occasion. We believe fashion is more than clothing — it is a statement of confidence, identity, and class.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, stylish, and well-crafted fashion pieces that meet the unique needs of our clients while delivering exceptional customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become a recognized luxury fashion brand known for elegance, creativity, and premium craftsmanship across Nigeria and beyond.

Why Choose RAD?

• Detailed craftsmanship • Clean and professional finishing • Bespoke luxury experience • Quality fabrics and styling • Customer-focused service
